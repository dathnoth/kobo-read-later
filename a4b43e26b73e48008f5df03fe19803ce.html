The News

Roblox promises to be less predatory because eSafety spent 2 years begging them to

If there's anyone between the ages of 5 and 15 in your life, you're probably aware of Roblox. It's extremely popular game - apparently 1.7m Australian kids signed up to play it. They just agreed to a court enforceable action from the eSafety Commissioner to make the following changes to the game: "preventing adults from contacting unknown children without parental consent, strengthening the account settings of children's accounts so they are private by default, accessible reporting mechanisms that notify users of complaint outcomes and engaging an independent third-party auditor to assess the effectiveness of its safety measures, including the deployment of its age-estimation measures". You know when there's a Wikipedia article dedicated purely to "child safety" on Roblox, shit is bad, so reluctantly, I will give eSafety credit for motivating Roblox to start giving a shit.

RBA blames AI boom for (some) inflation, Taiwan gives cash to each citizen because of AI boom

If you needed an additional reason to loathe AI, the RBA called it out as reason why they're struggling to get inflation into their target range, which has the consequence of higher interest rates. The deputy RBA governor said, "there's an extraordinary boom going on in the global economy in AI and the tech sector, and particularly in trading partners that we have. Anyone who's tried to buy a laptop recently will know that the price of memory for example has gone up sharply. That threatens inflation from overseas". Meanwhile, the Taiwanese government is giving every citizen NTD$10,000 (approx A$440) because the boom in demand for AI hardware - of which Taiwan is the manufacturing epicenter (TSMC, Foxconn, Quanta etc) - has been so lucrative for the country's economy. Things are so good right now in Taiwan, the political opposition reckons the cash payout should be double! Imagine if we did that for like, mining and resource extraction...

GrapheneOS still cooking with Motorola, even as Google makes life hard for them

GrapheneOS has posted on Mastodon with some info about the status of their partnership with Motorola - "initial devices with GrapheneOS support should be available in 2027. The initial devices will be flagships so they'll be higher end hardware than Pixels at a higher price. Lower end devices will take more time to meet our requirements since the updates and security features aren't as good. It's mostly due to how Qualcomm handles it. The latest Snapdragon flagships have the best security features. We'll also need Motorola to start paying them for longer updates below flagships". The same account also vented about how Google is making their life difficult on purpose by moving certain parts of Android's source code to Google Drive and accessible only on request, violating the GPL license the code is released under.
