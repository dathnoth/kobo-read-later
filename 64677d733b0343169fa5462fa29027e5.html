The News

MacBook Pros have touchscreens now, for the youth

Apple announced a touchscreen MacBook Pro as part of its week of new stuff. It's pretty much the same MacBook Pro we know and love, but the display is OLED, it weighs a little less, the Apple logo glows again (yay) and you can touch it like an iPad - but you can't fold it or spin the keyboard around or anything like that. They did redesign the hinge so it doesn't wobble when you paw at it though. When asked why they've done this now, after holding out for so long in an industry where all their competitors have multiple touchscreen/2-in-1 laptops, Apple's marketing boss Greg Joswiak told Wired "there's a generation of young creators and young people using our MacBook Pros that expect to touch". Not something I've ever wanted to do, but I am an old man so what do I know? I just hope it doesn't further enshittify macOS as they tweak it to support a touch interface.

Discord to make some servers open to search engines

Discord is going to make discussions in "Discoverable Servers" available to search engines as of November 16 this year. Discoverable Servers are Discord servers that have at least 1,000 members and anyone can join them, no invite required. There's 63,000 of these types of Discord servers apparently. Server admins can choose to opt-out and individual users can do the same. Kinda good this stuff is exposed to search engines are Discord servers are the web forums of this generation, with a lot of useful stuff stashed away in these proprietary silos. But I'll bet my house that Discord's incentive to do this is so they can get some of that sweet LLM money like Reddit, who has done deals with Google and OpenAI to sell real-time access to Reddit posts, worth hundreds of millions of dollars a year.

Amazon's still pumping out Alexa/Echo smart house gear

I don't know if anyone still cares what Amazon does with smart house stuff, but they flopped out a bunch of new Echo stuff overnight. There's a new Echo Dot, with a better speaker and an Eero wi-fi extender built-in. The Echo Studio is another orb shaped thingy that has "Amazon's best sounding speaker" in it, plus a camera so it has an "richer understanding of the room around it". The Echo Time is designed to be another orb (with a clock!) for your bedside table that'll "make waking up and winding down effortless" and helps you meet "evidence-based sleep standards". Alexa Photo Frame is a 10" digital photo fame but with Alexa deciding what goes on there when connected to Amazon Photos (I didn't even know Amazon had a photo thing) and the big boy Echo Show 16 is a big smart house display that now comes with a "nanomatte" screen to reduce reflections.

Something I Saw On The Internet

Sell your domain name via DNS

Here's a thing you could do with DNS I didn't know about: add a "_for-sale" TXT record to your domain and it lets everyone know you wanna flog your domain name off for cash. It's part of RFC 10023 and includes metadata for how to contact the seller and how much money they want for it. I assume this exists so brokers can do an automated scan of millions of domains, pick up the for sale record and then try find a buyer, clipping a little fee for themselves along the way. Imagine telling people this is your job - a domain name real estate agent. Humiliating.
