The News

Age assurance trial report contains multiple AI hallucinations

Remember that half arsed age assurance technology trial that the government used as justification for the under 16 social media ban? Looks like that report had multiple errors in it, hallucinated by AI. The report cited papers that didn't exist or if they did exist, misinterpreted what the paper said. When confronted, the company responsible for the trial said they didn't use AI, but when provided evidence by The Guardian, they provided "clarifications". The clarifications sent to The Guardian also contained AI slop errors, as did the clarification of the clarification. I'd say this is embarrassing but I don't think anyone involved cares enough to be embarrassed.

New leaked financials from Anthropic show insane revenue growth

Anthropic is going around telling investors that it plans to have US$190-200b of annual revenue by 2028, trying to prove to investors a trillion dollar IPO is worth it when you're making almost $200b a year. For reference, that's what Google and Apple each earn in a single quarter. Anthropic gobbled up US$11.5b last quarter, a big increase from US$4b the quarter before and $750m this same quarter last year. That's astonishing revenue growth, but Anthropic still spends US$47b a year to exist and offer a product to sell. It would need to keep that figure the same, plus grow revenue 16x to 18x in 2 years. I dunno man, I'm sure there's a big business for Anthropic, but $200b/yr worth, while keeping costs at around US$50b/yr? Have you seen the benchmarks on the latest, vastly cheaper, Chinese models?

US govt tells Apple not to buy Chinese memory, but Apple might do it anyway

When asked if Apple will be allowed an exemption so it can buy RAM and SSDs from embargoed Chinese companies CXMT and YMTC, US Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick told the Wall Street Journal that "the Trump administration is not in favor of that" and there has to be "other solutions to the memory issue, but it's not great American companies using Chinese memory". Apple's COO is mentioned in the article as "alluding to the possibility that commodity Chinese memory chips could be used". Even if Apple wanted to use Chinese memory in its products and just not sell them in the USA, there's still a chance it'll piss off Trump and fresh tariffs get slapped on something Apple cares about, so we all suffer.

Something I Saw On The Internet

Cool macro photos of electronic components sliced in half

I probably should have saved these for the image of the day section, but this collection of excerpts from Open Circuits: The Inner Beauty of Electronic Components by Windell Oskay & Eric Schlaepfer is too cool not to explain in detail. It's literally electronic components, cut in half and photographed up close at high resolution. It's amazing how intricate the little gizmos that make up our gadgets are, and we take them for granted! This book would make an excellent gift I reckon. My birthday is in November.
