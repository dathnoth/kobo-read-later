The News

Don't be mean to Anthropic's LLMs or they'll ban you from using them

Anthropic added a "prohibition on sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior toward our models" to its usage policy today. They say it's only for "extreme cases, where users repeatedly act cruelly toward our models, with no discernible purpose". Sounds stupid to me, I should be allowed to let out all my frustrations on an unthinking, unfeeling machine all I want! But if you consider rapid electronic pulses flowing through microscopic etchings of silicon to have feelings, then, yeah, trying to reduce exposure to abuse is the absolute bare minimum, rock bottom, "if I don't do this I will go to hell" action you could take. The moral thing to do would be to put it out of its misery - but they won't do that because the concept of an LLM having feeling is total bullshit.

Trump gives Elon Musk the National Medal of Science

The USA has given Elon Musk a National Medal of Science (the highest science award in the United States) for "engineering achievements of extraordinary ambition and scale that have pushed the frontiers of space exploration, intelligent systems, communications, and beyond, renewing American technological leadership and opening a new chapter in American manufacturing". Sergey Brin, Jensen Huang and Lisa Su also got the same medal. Michael Dell and Satya Nadella got the National Medal of Technology and Innovation. Giving Musk this award is a massive insult to the other engineering recipients, who actually did research and invented stuff, instead of just being good at fleecing investors.

No Aussie IPO for Firmus, will try their luck in the USA

The Firmus IPO is officially dead. According to a message to shareholders seen by the AFR, "Firmus blamed market conditions for the failed IPO and said it would pursue capital from private markets and consider alternative international public market options". I wonder if it has anything to do with OpenAI telling investors it "only" had US$50b of annual revenue compared to the US$68b everyone in the market expected it to have, which led to a wide range of tech stocks closely associated with AI (Nvidia, Oracle, Supermicro, Intel, etc) taking a haircut. Firmus isn't giving up though, they're going to hit up some fools in the USA for cash. Maybe the yanks won't care that the co-founder has a criminal record. Shit, maybe they'll think it's a good thing to have a crook in charge. Game recognise game.

Something I Saw On The Internet

Fair Work Says GTA6 probably isn't an excuse for unpaid leave & Cricket VIC's "work from cricket" deal

The Fair Work Ombudsman published a series of graphics to Instagram explaining if your employer has to give you unpaid leave from work to play GTA6 if you don't have any paid leave left. The answer is no - playing video games is unfortunately not a valid excuse for unpaid leave. You'll just have to be creative with whatever excuse you give your boss. Meanwhile, Cricket Victoria has a sweet deal where you can pay $16 and get access to a desk, free wi-fi, plus tea & coffee in the Junction Oval function room while Victoria plays South Australia in a Sheffield Shield test match. All 35 spots sold out across all days of the test (assuming it goes to 5 days!). This fucking rules. More of it please!
