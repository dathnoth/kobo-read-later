The News

Kmart's $89 smart glasses are raising many red flags

Electronic Frontiers Australia (EFA), Digital Rights Watch (DRW) and GetUp! have all launched campaigns against Kmart's $89 pervert smart glasses. The EFA said these glasses "are inherently unethical, highly intrusive, and in many practical applications, potentially unlawful under existing surveillance and other related legislation". DRW's blog post says that "smart glasses are pervasive secret surveillance, which means there's no way to consent to it. You can't negotiate with a form of harm you don't know is happening to you. People wearing smart glasses are showing the rest of us that they don't care about your consent" and that while "there may be a genuine use case for camera glasses as a visual aid for blind and low-vision people", "that isn't how they're being designed, marketed, or sold". Over 13,000 people signed GetUp's petition demanding Kmart stop selling the glasses.

Mainboards are the latest victim of AI-induced price rises

We all know that GPUs, HDDs, SSDs and RAM are impacted by AI hyperscalers gobbling up production, now there's news from Taiwan that even the humble consumer mainboard is going up in price. All the big brands (Asus, Gigabyte, etc) are being hit with huge increases in the cost of PCB manufacturing, along with raw materials like copper and the basic electronics that go on a mainboard, like capacitors. It also doesn't help these companies that demand for consumer mainboards is weak due to the fact all the bits that go into the mainboard (SSD, RAM, GPU) are so expensive. It's increasing the price of a product with weak demand is a bad combo for future growth! Building a PC is already a pretty niche thing to do, but I think that's gonna be a market that vanishes in the next few years and we will all be worse off for it.
