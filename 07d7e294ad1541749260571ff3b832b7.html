The News

Digital Duty of Care will make it possible to opt-out of social media algorithms

Draft legislation for the Digital Duty of Care was released today. It includes a "My Feed, My Way" initiative that'll force social media platforms "to send a notification to new and existing users offering them a choice over their default feed". Users can "choose to opt in to having their default feed include personalised content recommended by the algorithm" or "out of personally recommended content in their default feed and see the friends and creators they choose to follow". There's stuff in there about protecting kids under 18 from "harmful content" too, but the legal mandate to offer a feed without the rage-bait engagement shit is good stuff. IMHO that should be the default rather than an opt-out.

Huawei's new Mate XT 2 tri-fold phone is entirely free of USA supplied tech

Huawei announced a new tri-folding phone yesterday - the Mate XT 2. Not only is it a rare phone that can fold out to 10.2" screen, it's the first Huawei phone that has a CPU, GPU, NPU and modem all designed in-house by Huawei. The entire device is "free from US supply restrictions". Even the operating system, Harmony OS 7, is no longer reliant on Android APIs and is its own thing these days. Very cool phone and a nice fuck you to the USA. If the US government didn't impose import restrictions on Qualcomm in 2024 so they couldn't sell chips to Huawei, I doubt Huawei would have got to this level so quickly. An own goal from the USA I reckon.

Civ 7 is getting some awesome updates in 2027

Firaxis has big news for fans of the game Civilization (i.e: me) to celebrate 35 year sof the franchise. Arc of Tomorrow is a free update that introduces a new Atomic Age to the game, with "Expanded Nuclear gameplay", "Multilateral Agreements" and "Proxy Warfare". Old mate Ghandi (he loves a nuke or two) is back as well. Earthrise a bigger and paid expansion pack "themed around the historic space race and deploying missions across the solar system", that'll add various "space-themed gameplay mechanics in each Age". Both will be available in 2027, with Arc of Tomorrow coming first. Earthrise probably isn't going to be available until mid-2027. Both look like excellent additions to my favourite video game! BTW, Civ 7 is free this week on Steam (only a few days left) if you wanna try before you buy.

Something I Saw On The Internet

Xteink's mini e-readers are very on trend right now

The Atlantic has a nice piece about the Xteink X3, which if you don't already know, is a cheap (around $80-$100) 3.7" e-reader with an e-ink screen and magnetic back that makes it perfect for sticking to the back of your smartphone. Some people reckon due to the small size, they find themselves reading snippets of books, rather than using social media. The handful of words per "page" make it easier for some people to read than a "full" page of text too. It runs an ESP32, so it's very hackable, enabling stuff like this custom firmware that adds loads more features. There's a thread on The Sizzle forums about Xteink devices if you wanna poke around or ask questions.
