The News

OpenAI publishes messy blog post about its legal fight with Apple

OpenAI published a blog post titled "Apple is getting this wrong" giving their side of the disagreement they're having with Apple regarding theft of trade secrets. It includes iMessage screenshots and emails of conversations, with the claim that this is all Apple's fault because their systems (it looks like Apple employees have to use their personal iCloud accounts for confidential Apple files???) allowed it to happen and the accessing of Apple data by ex-Apple employees was actually at Apple's request. It appears that Apple making a new filing with the court is what prompted OpenAI's childish sounding post, with Apple wanting more info from more people at OpenAI as in its initial digging around after getting access to documents from OpenAI, it found more ex-Apple employees at OpenAI it thinks are complicit.

ACCC to investigate if telcos should be forced to do domestic roaming

The ACCC is going to spend 12 months deciding if it should "declare one or more wholesale mobile services, including domestic roaming and a radio access network service". Declare, in this legal ACCC jargon means that "a mobile provider would have to give other providers access to services using its infrastructure or network on terms regulated by the ACCC" - i.e: Telstra could have to give MVNOs access to the full network (not a cut-down one that excludes the more remote regions) and/or allow other telcos to roam onto its full network, similar to what TPG and Optus do now and what the ACCC blocked Telstra and TPG from doing a few years ago. The inquiry will also consider if regional customers have "meaningful choice" between providers and the role low earth orbit satellites like Starlink play with direct-to-device services.

Big Walk is out now and critics love it

Big Walk - Melbourne game studio House House's follow up to Untitled Goose Game - went on sale today and it's already achieved a Metacritic score of 94/100 and is the top selling global game on Steam by revenue, an impressive feat considering it's only $22. I liked Aftermath's description of Big Walk: "basic premise of Big Walk is that you wander a realistic-looking open world that's dotted here and there with brightly-colored cartoon buildings. These buildings are puzzles that can't be solved on your own, and they reward you with what my group decided was a red bean. If you bring enough beans to what my group came to call a Bean House, you unlock a key that opens up new features of the world". It's totally multi-player and I do not like multi-player games, but I really like what House House does, so I'll give it a shot.

Something I Saw On The Internet

If you're in the market for Zigbee temp sensors, read this

Smart Home Scene has a great roundup of cheap display-less Zigbee temperature sensors, the kind you find on Temu or AliExpress. Most of them perform roughly the same, but the differences come in the type of physical form factor, how they're powered and crucially, how reliable they are in reporting data back to your Zigbee hub. Surprisingly, they're all pretty good! I would have expected a few duds in the sub-$15 price range. The reviewer liked the Sonoff SNZB-02P best, which based on my experience with Sonoff gear, I can believe that it would be the best.
