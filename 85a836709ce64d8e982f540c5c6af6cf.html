The News

Micron tells investors RAM is gonna be "tight" until 2028 at least

Micron's CEO told investors today that "in calendar 2027 as well as 2028, we see demand exceeding supply. In fact, we see greater tightness in the industry in 2027 and in 2028 versus 2026. Overall, supply-demand environment is only getting tighter" and that "we do not have line of sight to when supply and demand will return to balance". On the same day, Raspberry Pi announced price increases for 2GB variants of the Pi 5 and Pi 4, with a suggestion to use the older Pi 3 instead if it's appropriate. Pretty sure I say the same thing every time there's some RAM price news, but this sucks. The cost of RAM is going to kill the build your own PC market I reckon. Only a niche (rich people) within a niche (people that build their own PCs) can justify the cost of an SSD and RAM to build the rest of a new PC around.

Ampol buys Evie EV fast charging network to create Australia's biggest

A few people let me know about this - Ampol has purchased Evie for $225 million. All 1,030 of Evie's charging bays will join Ampol's network, making it the biggest fast/DC charging network in Australia with 1,425 bays across more than 400 sites. Nice exit for Evie's investors (primarily Trevor St. Baker, who took a massive loss on Tritium) that were shoving cash into the business for years without making a profit. Check out the presentation Ampol provided to investors explaining why they purchased Evie. A very interesting stat is that they sold an average of 142kWh of energy per bay, per day, in September, it was 117kWh during FY2026 and 134kWh in June 2026. Just goes to show how rapidly EV uptake is happening. The business will make $30m of EBITDA (aka profit-ish) within 3 years. Not bad and now I'm really annoyed none of my EV startups took off.

Amazon has new Kindles and is still trying to make drone deliveries a thing here

Amazon's got new Kindles. I won't go through all the changes here, but the basic Kindle, the Kindle Paperwhite and the Kindle Colorsoft all got pretty nice spec bumps. Faster operation, more battery life, slightly lighter, etc. Apparently they've all been redesigned "from the ground up" and "every device now features a flush-front display", as well as new colours. There's new accessories too, like a Bluetooth remote, a cover with physical buttons (these new Kindles don't have any) and a charging dock. I love a good charging dock. On the topic of Amazon, they're still digging away at making drone deliveries happen in Australia, seeking a new "head of regulatory approvals" to deal with CASA and Airservices Australia. I could have sworn they gave up on the whole drone thing. I guess not.

Something I Saw On The Internet

A memorial for Ukrainian IT professionals killed in the war

Here's a sobering website for your Friday afternoon - "a project by the DOU editorial team in honor of fallen IT professionals in the Russo-Ukrainian War since 2014. Here you will find brief biographies of military personnel who gave their lives for Ukraine, as well as civilians killed by Russian forces". Imagine if, I dunno, Indonesia or China or the USA decided to take parts of Australia and a bunch of us nerds had to help defend the country in our own unique ways. There's a good chance someone you know would end up on a site like this that someone we know would probably create. It feels so far fetched for me, but for our fellow computer touchers in Ukraine, it's a very harsh and terrifying reality. I keep scrolling and scrolling, there's so many needlessly dead people.
