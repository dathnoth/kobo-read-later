The Sizzle - Saturday 7th November

Hello everyone!

For over 11 years The Sizzle arrived in your inbox every week day. Starting on the 9th of November 2026, The Sizzle will be sent twice a week instead of daily. Every Monday and every Thursday, you'll get the same Sizzle you know and love, but with 5 bits of news, 3 interesting things I saw on the internet, an image of the day and a couple of bargains.

Why am I making this change? I'm finding it difficult to write about the current state of this wretched industry in just a paragraph each day. News stories on an issue or topic now trickle out over a few days and require more background to fully explain and provide proper context in which they exist.

By slowing down and writing every Monday and Thursday, I can give you that context in a way that's sustainable for me. The news will still be concise and to the point, but now I can add a few more sentences for a more holistic view of a story, that I reckon will be of way more value and is what I would want to read if I was on the other side of the keyboard.

I also want to avoid putting myself in the same situation as 2024 when I was pissed off with The Sizzle and quit. I'm hoping that going from daily, to twice a week, is a healthy balance of wanting to start the Butlerian Jihad but still writing about the nerd stuff I genuinely love and would still be monitoring even if nobody was paying me.

The Sizzle is still $60/yr or $6/m. There's still no ads, affiliate links, sponsored content or link tracking. The RSS feed and forum aren't going anywhere. I'll still never use AI to write or even assist with writing The Sizzle. You can still cancel your subscription at any time without jumping through hoops and get an automatic, immediate prorated refund back to your card.

Thanks to the 1,303 of you that pay to The Sizzle. It means so much to me and I really hope that you like the twice a week Sizzle format. As always, my email inbox is open :-)
--Anthony
